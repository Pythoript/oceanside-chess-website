Simul with
GM Samy Shoker

European Middleweight Chess Boxing World Champion
FIDE 2500 GM · Peak Blitz 2600 · San Diego, California

Oceanside Chess is proud to welcome Grandmaster Samy Shoker from San Diego, California — the reigning European Middleweight Chess Boxing World Champion — to Vancouver Island for an exclusive 50-board simultaneous exhibition. This is a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity to face a Grandmaster up close and learn from one of the most unique talents in the game.

Meet GM Samy Shoker

Chess Grandmaster. Chess Boxing World Champion. Samy Shoker isn't your typical GM. When he's not grinding out wins at 2600 blitz Elo, he's stepping into the ring as the European Middleweight Chess Boxing World Champion — a sport that alternates rounds of speed chess with rounds of boxing. Based in San Diego, Samy brings that same intensity and showmanship to every board he touches. Now he's bringing it to Vancouver Island for the very first time.

Included with Entry

Stay for the Lecture

After the simul, GM Shoker will gather the group for an optional one-hour session where he'll walk through key positions from the games, break down his decision-making in real time, and take your questions. It's a rare chance to go inside the mind of a Grandmaster while the games are still fresh — stick around!

🧠 GM thought process revealed
