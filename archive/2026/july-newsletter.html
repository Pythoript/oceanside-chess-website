Meet GM Samy Shoker

Chess Grandmaster. Chess Boxing World Champion. Samy isn't your typical GM. When he's not grinding out wins at 2600 blitz Elo, he's stepping into the ring as the European Middleweight Chess Boxing World Champion — a sport that alternates rounds of speed chess with rounds of boxing. Based in San Diego, Samy brings that same intensity and showmanship to every board he touches. Now he's bringing it to Vancouver Island for the very first time.

Stay for the 2-Hour Lecture

After the simul, GM Shoker will gather the group for a deep-dive session. He'll walk through key positions from the day's games, break down his real-time decision-making, and take your questions. It's a rare chance to go inside the mind of a Grandmaster while the games are still fresh!

  • 🔍 Game analysis & key positions from the simul
  • 🧠 GM thought process revealed in real time
  • 🙋 Open Q&A session — ask anything!
