Club Moves to Parksville Library

Hello Everyone!

As the holiday season approaches, we’d like to inform you that

our club is now closed for winter break. Our next meeting won't be until Saturday, January 10th, 2026.

We are excited to share that when we do return, it will be at our new location in Parksville! We have moved from St. Marks Church to the Parksville Library with meetings being held next to the computer area.

Starting in 2026, we will also be reducing our meetings to 3 times a month, with the club being closed the first Saturday of every month.

We apologize for any inconvenience these changes may cause; however, we believe they are the best path forward in ensuring the club’s long-term sustainability. Following these changes, we will also be focusing more on our club’s programs and events by

reallocating funds from venue costs directly back into our services, ensuring a brighter future for our community.

As always, we thank you for your continued support, and wish you and your families a wonderful and restful holiday season!
